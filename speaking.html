Speaking & Events

On stage and in the field.

Moderating, facilitating and speaking on media, gender leadership and social change, from the TEDx stage and the UN General Assembly to community workshops.

01 — Featured talk · TEDxPashaStreet

Social Media for Social Change.

A talk connecting her humanitarian work with media strategy, drawing on the projects behind her 2026 JCI TOYP recognition.

Beyond passive consumption

Digital and social media must shift from tools of passive entertainment into active mechanisms for learning, civic engagement and societal transformation.

Childhood anchors

Drawing on the psychology of childhood communication, such as simple songs and stories, she showed how people learn and process trauma.

The strategy behind the success

Social and Behaviour Change Communication (SBCC) drives her work. The success of Kids Lifeline and I Am the President is not random: it comes from analysing audience data, demographics and habits to shape reality TV, radio and podcasts that turn audiences into active change-makers.

In times of conflict and crisis, strategic, trusted media is not a luxury. It is a lifeline.
